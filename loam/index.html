Circular and new-economy projects that already exist, and are already working.

    Each flower is a project, planted in the year it began and taller the longer it has kept going. Press play to watch the garden grow, or drag through the years. A gold ring marks the ones you kept.

    Every project here was running at the time of writing (2026). Figures are rounded and as last reported by the project or the press; the project’s own site has current numbers.
