How this works

Property prices and holiday-rental figures are the latest published data I could find per spot (RealAdvisor, INE, AirROI and others, mostly September–October 2026; each spot lists its sources). Wind, sun and swell are measured live on your phone from three years of hourly weather-model data (Open-Meteo, 2023–2025), so they arrive a few seconds after you open the app and are then kept on this phone. Model wind reads a little low at funnel spots like Tarifa and Sotavento, so compare spots with each other rather than trusting the absolute day count.

Prices are medians: cheaper flats exist, which is what the “below median” chips are for. Rental figures are market averages, scaled down for small units. Taxes are simplified. I’m not a tax adviser, so check with a local gestor or notary before you buy.

Your budget and choices are saved on this phone. Weather requests send only each spot’s public coordinates to Open-Meteo.
