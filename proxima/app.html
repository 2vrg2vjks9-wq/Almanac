PROXIMA

Tomorrow’s inventions, explained with today’s science.

Wow numbers are real measurements or real estimates from today’s science, not predictions.

Readiness uses NASA’s Technology Readiness Level scale as it stands today: TRL 1 means the basic principle has been observed, TRL 4 a lab prototype works, TRL 9 the thing has flown or shipped.

Plausibility says what stands in the way. Engineering means known physics suffices and it is a matter of cost and effort. Breakthrough means at least one scientific discovery is still missing. Frontier means it sits at the edge of known physics and may never work.

First light is a guess at the year the first working version appears. The “Seeds today” notes describe real research.
